Recording a donation (admins)

  1. Pledged — in NetBox, install a Planned module of the promised part in the empty bay and set its Donor field. It appears here as pledged and on the needs page as a specific ask.
  2. Installed — once the part is in, the next hardware_inventory.py run replaces the planned module with the real one and carries Donor / Donated On over.
  3. Already installed — set Donor and Donated On directly on the module (or on the device, for a whole chassis).
