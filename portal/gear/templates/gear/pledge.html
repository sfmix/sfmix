What happens next

  1. We check the fit. A volunteer emails you to confirm the part matches the slot it's meant for (speed, form factor, caddy).
  2. Drop-off or shipping. We'll work out a hand-off at one of our sites, or shipping.
  3. It goes into service. We install it and record it against its slot, with your name on it.
  4. You're on the wall. The donor wall lists you, what you gave and the machines it runs in.
