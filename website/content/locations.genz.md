## where to pull up 📍

to connect, drop a cross-connection order with the facility operator at your chosen facility. cross-connect pricing is set by the facility operator. *SFMIX does not pay for participant-facing cross-connects.* (that's on you, no shade 🤷.)

all locations offer **10, 100, or 400Gbps** fiber connectivity. hella fast fr ⚡.

*single-mode fiber only (10km 1310nm optics). no multi-mode fiber or copper. BiDi available for 10G and 100G.*

not in one of the buildings below? no stress. SFMIX is also available from buildings next door (listed under each spot). at 200 Paul, SFMIX is also reachable over a passive DWDM mux in a nearby suite (tunable or colored DWDM optics required).
