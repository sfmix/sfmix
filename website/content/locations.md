## Where to Connect

To connect, place a cross-connection order with the facility operator at your chosen facility. Cross-connect pricing is set by the facility operator. *SFMIX does not pay for participant-facing cross-connects.*

All locations offer **10, 100, or 400Gbps** fiber connectivity.

*Single-mode fiber only (10km 1310nm optics). No multi-mode fiber or copper. BiDi available for 10G and 100G.*

You don't have to be in one of the buildings below. SFMIX is also reachable from:

- **Any CoreSite building in the region:** CoreSite transports cross-connects to SFMIX.
- **Any Equinix campus in the region:** Equinix transports cross-connects to SFMIX.
- **The Astound suite at 200 Paul:** over a passive DWDM mux to SFMIX (tunable or colored DWDM optics required).
- **Lumen, next to H5 Silicon Valley:** across the parking lot.
