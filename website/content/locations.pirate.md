## Where to Climb Aboard

To climb aboard, place a cross-connection order with th' meet-me room (MMR) operator at yer chosen port o' call. Cross-connect pricing be set by th' MMR operator. *SFMIX does not pay for matey-facing cross-connects.*

All ports o' call offer **10, 100, or 400Gbps** fiber connectivity.

*Single-mode fiber only (10km 1310nm optics). No multi-mode fiber nor copper. Tunable or colored DWDM optics required in th' Astound suite (passive MUX to SFMIX). BiDi available for 10G an' 100G.*
