Three takes on a /team/ page where more of the team fits on one screen. Operations comes first, since those are the people participants deal with day to day, and the board follows.

All three read from the same records. A role is metadata on a person rather than the section they're filed under, so Matt Peterson is one entry that shows up under both operations and the board:

  • AEveryone at a glanceEvery person appears once, on one screen. Role filters light people up instead of hiding the rest, so picking Board lights Matt up in the operations row too.
  • BWho does whatA people-by-roles grid. A line joins each person's roles, so overlaps like general manager plus director are easy to spot. Click a column to filter by that role.
  • COperations firstMatt leads a featured operations section, with a panel for reaching the team. The board follows as one strip of faces. Matt appears in both places, linked to each other.

How this would land in Hugo

The bios in content/team.md move into website/data/team.yaml, and a team layout ranges over it, filtered by role. Translations keep their prose in team.<lang>.md or in per-language data files.
